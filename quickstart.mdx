## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A valid email address for account creation
- A modern web browser like Chrome or Firefox
- Optional: GitHub account for version control integration

</Callout>

## Get Started in Minutes

Follow these steps to set up your Lodestone Consulting Ltd documentation workspace and publish your first page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://docs.lodestoneconsulting.com/signup` and create your account.

    Enter your email, name, and a secure password. Verify your email to activate the account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Workspace" icon="folder-plus">
    From the dashboard, click **New Workspace**.

    Name it `Lodestone-Docs` and select **Public** visibility for sharing. Confirm creation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add First Document" icon="file-plus">
    In your workspace, select **New Page** and title it `Introduction`.

    Paste sample content or use the built-in editor to add your first markdown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Organize Structure" icon="layers">
    Create folders like `guides/`, `api/`, and `changelog/` via the sidebar.

    Drag and drop pages into folders for a clean hierarchy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and Publish" icon="eye">
    Click **Preview** to view live rendering.

    Hit **Publish** to make it live. Share the generated URL with your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Basic Organization Tips

Organize your docs effectively from the start.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Folder Structure" icon="folder">
    ```
    docs/
    ├── introduction.mdx
    ├── quickstart.mdx
    ├── services/
    │   └── mind-flow.mdx
    └── changelog.mdx
    ```
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Navigation" icon="menu">
    Use frontmatter for automatic sidebar generation:

    ````mdx
    ---
    title: Services
    sidebar_label: MIND/FLOW™
    ---
    ````
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
