---
title: Can't Log In to Dashboard
description: Fix login issues for Lodestone Consulting Ltd dashboard.
---

## Symptoms

- Login page shows "Invalid credentials" or freezes
- Can't access your projects

## Causes

- Wrong email or password
- Account not activated
- Browser cache issues

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check credentials">
    Make sure your email and password are correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate your account">
    Click the activation link sent to your email after registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Try logging in after clearing cookies and cache.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    Use "Forgot password?" on the login page to set a new one.
  </Step>
</Steps>
