---

## Overview

Organize your business documentation efficiently.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new project">
    Log in to your dashboard. Click "New Project" and enter project details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documentation pages">
    Use the editor to create new pages, or start from a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize sections">
    Drag and drop pages to arrange your section hierarchy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite collaborators">
    Go to the Team tab and invite editors or viewers to work together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and review">
    Save your changes. Share with your team for feedback.
  </Step>
</Steps>
